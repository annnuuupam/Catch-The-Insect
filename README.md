# 🕹️ Catch The Insect v2

**Catch The Insect** is a fast-paced, interactive web game where you can challenge yourself to catch various virtual insects as they frantically appear on your screen. Choose your "favorite" bug, test your reflexes against the clock, and see if you can achieve a new High Score!

## 🌟 Game Overview

In this fully revamped version, you are racing against a strict 60-second timer. Catching insects grants you points, but the more points you get, the smaller and harder they are to catch! Watch out for the elusive **Golden Bug** to score huge bonus points!


## 🎮 Key Features

- **Progressive Difficulty Levels**: As you hit point thresholds (10, 20, 40), the game upgrades your level, and bugs spawn progressively smaller.
- **60-Second Countdown**: Replaced the endless timer loop with a race-against-the-clock format for maximum replayability.
- **The Golden Bug**: A 10% chance for a rare "Golden" variant of your insect to spawn. Catching it rewards you with +5 Points!
- **High Score Tracking**: Your absolute best score is automatically saved locally in your browser's `localStorage` and persists between play sessions.
- **Pause & Resume**: Need a break? Hit the **`P`** key or **`Spacebar`** at any time to pause the timer, freeze bug spawning, and bring up a sleek Pause menu.
- **Premium UI Overhaul**: Features a dynamic, animated multi-stop gradient background and gorgeous frosted glassmorphism overlays instead of flat colors.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **HTML5**: Structuring the robust multi-screen interface and modals.
- **CSS3**: Powering the animated gradients, glassmorphism filters, keyframe pulse animations, and responsiveness.
- **Vanilla JavaScript**: Managing global game states, intervals, keyboard events (`keydown`), `localStorage` persistence, and algorithmic bug spawning.

## 🚀 How to Play

1. **Start the Game**: Click the "Play Game" button. You can also view the "How to Play" rules directly from the main screen.
2. **Choose Your Insect**: Pick your target—Fly, Mosquito, Spider, or Roach.
3. **The Hunt Begins**: You have 60 Seconds. Click on the bugs as fast as they appear!
4. **Golden Bugs**: Prioritize catching the glowing golden bugs for massive point boosts!
5. **Level Up**: Keep your aiming sharp—the higher you score, the smaller the insects become.
6. **Time's Up**: When the clock hits zero, your final score is tallied against your High Score. Hit "Play Again" to restart!

## 📁 Project Structure

- `index.html` - The structural backbone establishing the Start, Selection, and Gameplay container screens.
- `style.css` - Responsible for the premium animated backgrounds, typography, and beautiful overlays.
